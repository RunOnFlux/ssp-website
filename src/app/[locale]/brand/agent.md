Official logos, symbol, icons, colors, typography and usage guidelines for SSP,
SSP Wallet (browser extension) and SSP Key (mobile app). Meant for articles,
integrations, exchange and swap-provider listings, and videos. The same files
live in the public repository https://github.com/RunOnFlux/ssp-brand-kit.

## Key facts

- Full kit (ZIP):
  https://github.com/RunOnFlux/ssp-brand-kit/archive/refs/heads/master.zip
- SVG is the source for every logo; PNGs have transparent backgrounds.
- Logo (symbol + SSP wordmark), 2048 px wide PNGs:
  - On dark (default): https://sspwallet.io/brand/logo/ssp-logo-on-dark.svg
  - On light: https://sspwallet.io/brand/logo/ssp-logo-on-light.svg
  - Black (on SSP Amber, one-color print):
    https://sspwallet.io/brand/logo/ssp-logo-black.svg
  - White (photos, busy or colored backgrounds):
    https://sspwallet.io/brand/logo/ssp-logo-white.svg
  - Each also as .png at the same path.
- Symbol (three pillars only), 1024 px tall PNGs:
  https://sspwallet.io/brand/symbol/ssp-symbol-{on-dark,on-light,black,white}.svg
- Icon (symbol on a solid tile), SVG plus PNG at 1024 and 256 px:
  https://sspwallet.io/brand/icon/ssp-icon-{circle,square}-{amber,dark,light}.svg
  (PNG: same name with .png, or -256.png).
- For provider, swap and wallet lists use the amber circle icon:
  https://sspwallet.io/brand/icon/ssp-icon-circle-amber.svg
- SSP Key app icon: https://sspwallet.io/brand/icon/ssp-key-app-icon-512.png
- Colors:
  - SSP Amber #FBBF24 (primary)
  - Amber Light #FCD34D (center pillar)
  - Amber Deep #F59E0B (left pillar, hover)
  - Amber Action #D97706 (calls to action)
  - Graphite #595A5A (right pillar and wordmark on light backgrounds)
  - Night #0C0A09 (dark backgrounds)
  - White #FFFFFF (right pillar and wordmark on dark backgrounds)
- Text and icons on SSP Amber are always black, never white.
- Typography: Montserrat (headings, 600–800), Inter (body and UI, 400–700).
  The SSP wordmark is custom lettering; never retype it.
- Clear space: one pillar width (x) on every side of the logo.
- Minimum sizes: logo 96 px / 25 mm wide, symbol 16 px / 5 mm tall,
  icon 24 px / 8 mm.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolor, add effects to, lower the contrast of, or
  retype the logo.
- Naming: always "SSP" in capitals; products are "SSP Wallet" and "SSP Key".
  Tagline: "Secure. Simple. Powerful."
- The SSP name, logos and product names are trademarks of InFlux Technologies
  Limited. The assets are not licensed as open source.
- Brand, press and partnership contact: info@sspwallet.io
